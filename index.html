HW 09 · Orchestration

Agent Orchestration: Demand → Reorder

Problem
Connect a demand-sensing agent to a reorder agent so forecasts drive stock decisions automatically.
Built
Two agents in Claude Code handing off through a shared data file; batch runs across forecast rounds; live dashboards on GitHub Pages and Google Sheets.
Finding
A 4-week reorder point fails with monthly inventory checks. Covering lead time, the review gap and buffer needs about 8.3 weeks.

HW 08 · Demand sensing

The Demand-Sensing Agent

Problem
Forecast monthly sales from weather, customer reviews and industry news.
Built
Multiple regression on temperature, a review-sentiment score and a news index, refit each round from a Google Drive workbook, plus a shared dashboard tab.
Finding
R² settled near 0.98 at 12 months. Forecast misses of +145 and −109 units (about 10%) came from proxy inputs, not the model.
HW 07 · Forecasting

Demand Forecasting Accuracy

Problem
Pick a forecasting method for 24 months of growing demand.
Built
3-period moving average vs. exponential smoothing (α = 0.3), scored on MAD and MSE in a spreadsheet.
Finding
Moving average won (about 17% lower MAD, 44% lower MSE), but tracking signals of +13 and +17 showed both were biased low. Recommended a trend-adjusted method.
HW 06 · Agent design

Warehouse Data-to-Dashboard Agent

Problem
Turn a manual data-cleaning and KPI workflow into a reliable AI agent.
Built
Agent role, tool list, guardrails and four human approval checkpoints; 6-month KPI trend dashboard.
Finding
Warehouse D's volume more than doubled while its on-time rate fell from 55% to 36%. Damage rate rose from 5% to 8%.
HW 05 · Customer analytics

Voice of the Customer

Problem
Find which supply chain failures drive customer complaints.
Built
AI classification of reviews into seven themes across three datasets, with human correction of miscategorized reviews.
Finding
Shipping/delivery led every dataset (27–28%). About half of all complaints trace back to the distribution center.
HW 04 · Network design

Distribution Network Optimization

Problem
Choose unified or segmented distribution for product lines with different demand patterns.
Built
24-week cost models per segment under tiered and flat pricing.
Finding
A mixed network matched to each segment's volatility cut cost about 10.6% vs. all-unified and 6.9% vs. all-segmented.
HW 03 · Capacity

Bottlenecks & Capacity Investment

Problem
Close a throughput gap of 355 vs. 400 units/hr on a $3,500 budget.
Built
Theory of Constraints analysis across parallel lines, with investment sequencing.
Finding
Funding the cheapest bottleneck first reaches about 411–414 units/hr, with a 2.5% buffer before the next constraint takes over.
HW 02 · Data & KPIs

Warehouse Operations Dashboard

Problem
Make a messy two-table order export usable for decisions.
Built
Data audit, rule-based cleaning (flag, never guess), 12 KPIs and an Excel dashboard with conditional formatting.
Finding
On-time rate was 52%. Warehouse D handled 11 of 25 orders at 36% on time.
HW 01 · Transportation

Transportation Mode Selection

Problem
Pick truck, rail or air for each lane under delivery deadlines.
Built
Fixed + variable cost models per lane, filtered by transit time.
Finding
Deadlines, not distance, drove cost. Moving one lane from 2 to 3 days would save $12,400 by switching air to truck.
